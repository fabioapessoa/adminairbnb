A Few Things to Know Before Your Stay

Please review our property guidelines to help ensure a comfortable experience for everyone.

Check-in & check-out

Check-in is after 4:00 PM and check-out is before 10:00 AM, with self check-in through a smart lock.

Occupancy

Up to 8 overnight guests, including children. Only registered guests may stay or use shared amenities.

No smoking

Smoking and vaping are not permitted indoors. A $1,250 fee applies for ozone air treatment if this occurs.

Pets

Well-behaved pets are welcome, one per stay. Please keep pets off the furniture and clean up after them.

Quiet hours

9:00 PM to 9:00 AM. This is a peaceful, year-round residential community — thank you for keeping noise low.

No parties or events

Gatherings beyond the registered guests on the booking are not permitted.

Parking

One assigned spot is included. A second vehicle must be confirmed with us in advance and isn't guaranteed. No golf carts or trailers.

Pool access

A pool access key is provided for your stay. A $100 fee applies if it's lost or not returned.

Beach gear

Chairs, an umbrella and a cooler are provided — please rinse and return them to their storage spot after use.

No commercial filming

Commercial photography or filming is not permitted on the property.

Accessibility

This is a second-floor duplex reached by stairs, with an additional staircase to the bedrooms. It is not wheelchair accessible.

Your information is used only to prepare for your stay and to meet the condominium's security requirements.
